# Interface

The side panel always describes what is under the cursor:

- On a bare tile: terrain effects and move costs.
- On a unit: full stats for that unit.
- With one of your units selected and an enemy hovered: a **shot preview**.

The shot preview shows the hit chance with every modifier that produced it,
the damage against the armour facing the shot would strike, and whether the
target can return fire.
