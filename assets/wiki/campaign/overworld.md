# Overworld

The campaign map is the strategic layer: armies, capturable objectives,
and softer fog than battle.

## Armies and contact

Armies move day by day. Moving onto a visible enemy army starts a battle;
survivors and casualties write back to the campaign.

Eligible neighbours may join — see [Massed Battles](../battle/massed-battles.md).

## Objectives and income

Objectives on the map can be captured. Controlled tiles feed income to
their owning side's funds.

## Strategic fog

Overworld fog is softer than battle fog. Armies are visible to everyone
unless they sit in concealing terrain with no enemy adjacent. Hide in
forests and keep distance if you need to disappear on the march.
