# Massed Battles

A clash is rarely just two armies. Anyone standing next to the contested
tile may join, so a battle can pull five armies rather than two.

| Role | Cost to join |
| --- | --- |
| Attacker | Spends their turn — joining an assault is an action |
| Defender | Free — holding ground you already stand on is not |

The player picks from a muster prompt (keys `1–9`, `M` add all, `N` drop
all). The AI commits everything eligible.

After the fight, each army gets its own survivors back. Casualties do not
bleed into armies that sat the battle out.
