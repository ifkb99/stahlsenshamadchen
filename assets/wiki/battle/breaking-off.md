# Breaking Off

Eight rounds with no damage dealt and nobody holding an enemy in sight
ends the battle as a draw. Both sides keep what they have.

Without this rule, survivors who lose each other in the fog would wander
until they happened to collide — hundreds of rounds — with the campaign
stuck behind them.

Contact counts as much as damage, and it is checked every tick of a
[round](the-round.md). A long, careful approach under observation is never
mistaken for a stalemate: as long as someone still sees the enemy, the
fight continues.
