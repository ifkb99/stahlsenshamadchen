# A fight on the ground: the player sends her 1st Company against the
# Valkyrie Vanguard, the generated campaign runs on the clock until they
# make contact, the fight screen opens on it, and she commits minute after
# minute until it is over — the fight resolved by the world clock, in
# lockstep with the campaign.
#
# She starts it herself. The tour used to wait for the Valkyries to come,
# and whether they come for a company that sits still is a fact about the
# campaign AI and the ground (since W6.5 they do not, on seed 1), not about
# fighting on the ground, which is what this is for.
#!env STAHL_CAMPAIGN=frontier_world STAHL_WORLD=default
#
#   STAHL_CAMPAIGN=frontier_world STAHL_DEBUG=1 \
#     STAHL_SCRIPT=scripts/dev/fight-on-the-ground.txt cargo run -p stahlsenshamädchen

at 3.0
until idle 10
hex "1st Company"
wait 0.3
click left
wait 0.3
hex "Valkyrie Vanguard"
wait 0.3
key KeyG
until log "1st Company ordered to advance on" 10
key BracketRight
key BracketRight
key BracketRight
key Space
until log "The fighting is waiting for your orders." 400
wait 3.0
shot /tmp/fight-01-opened.png
press Enter until log "The fighting is over." 500
shot /tmp/fight-02-over.png
# Back on the campaign screen, the clock stopped where the fight left it.
until log "Clock" 30
wait 2.0
shot /tmp/fight-03-back.png
quit
