# What the order keys mean, said before they are pressed.
#
#   STAHL_PRESENT=immediate STAHL_DEBUG=1 STAHL_BATTLE=river_crossing \
#     STAHL_SCRIPT=scripts/dev/orders-explained.txt cargo run -p stahlsenshamädchen
#
# The complaint this tour photographs: `G` and `X` both send a platoon at a
# hex, one of them halts the moment anybody shoots, and until this the game
# said so nowhere at all. A player who meant "attack" pressed the movement to
# contact and watched her attack stop halfway.
#
# Hexes are axial. river_crossing places side 0's medium tank at offset
# [10, 20] -> axial (0, 20).

at 3.0

# The unit panel first. Selecting a crew should now say what a click means
# and what X means, because the per-crew twin of the assault order was
# reachable by keyboard and explained in the changelog and nowhere else.
hex 0,20
wait 0.4
click left
wait 0.5
log "crew selected: the panel offers both marches"
shot /tmp/orders-01-crew.png

# Then the formation panel, which is where the mission verbs live. F picks a
# formation and drops the unit selection; the menu is the bottom half of the
# page.
key Escape
wait 0.3
key F
wait 0.5
log "formation picked: the order menu is up"
shot /tmp/orders-02-menu.png

# Give one, and read it back. The standing order now carries its own price on
# the line under it, so a player who comes back to this platoon three rounds
# later can still tell which of the two orders she gave.
hex 8,24
wait 0.4
key X
wait 0.6
expect log "ordered to assault"
shot /tmp/orders-03-assault-read-back.png

# ...and the same platoon under the other verb, for the comparison. This is
# the pair of screenshots to look at: the two pages should differ in the
# clause under `Orders:`, not only in one word of the sentence.
key G
wait 0.6
expect log "ordered to advance"
shot /tmp/orders-04-advance-read-back.png

quit
