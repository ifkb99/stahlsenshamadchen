# A tour of the chain of command on screen: picking a formation, giving it a
#!env STAHL_BATTLE=river_crossing STAHL_SEED=7
# mission, and watching the order travel down the wire.
#
#   STAHL_PRESENT=immediate STAHL_DEBUG=1 STAHL_BATTLE=river_crossing \
#     STAHL_SCRIPT=scripts/dev/command-tour.txt cargo run -p stahlsenshamädchen
#
# Hexes are axial. river_crossing's side 0 is the player's, in two formations:
# 1st Armored Platoon (medium tank at offset [10, 20] -> axial (0, 20), light
# tank at [11, 26]) and the Reconnaissance Section. The bridge — the ground
# the whole map is about — is offset [20, 20] -> axial (10, 20).

at 3.0
log "planning phase, nobody selected"
shot /tmp/command-01-planning.png

# F walks the player's formations. Its members light up violet and the panel
# becomes the formation panel: leader, standing orders, membership.
key F
wait 0.6
log "1st Armored Platoon selected"
shot /tmp/command-02-formation.png

# F again for the Reconnaissance Section, which is where the net is worth
# looking at: Elsa Brandt leads it from a recon car, and her signals buy her
# hexes her platoon commander does not have — so the panel's "Net:" line
# carries the crew's share of it, and the green ring on the map is drawn at
# the further radius. Both numbers come out of BattleState::radio_reach, the
# same function the contact graph walks, so the ring is the edge rather than
# a picture of one.
key F
wait 0.6
log "the Reconnaissance Section: a wider net, drawn and named"
shot /tmp/command-03-net.png

# Round the cycle and back: F past the last formation drops the mode
# entirely, so two more presses put the 1st Armored Platoon in hand again for
# the order below.
key F
wait 0.3
key F
wait 0.4

# Aim at the bridge and order the platoon onto it. The order goes out through
# Order::SetMission, so MissionAssigned reaches the log exactly as it does
# when the enemy's commander speaks — and the panel lists the mission as in
# the air, because the base mod prices a signals net.
hex 10,20
wait 0.4
key G
wait 1.2
log "advance ordered on the bridge"
shot /tmp/command-04-mission.png

# Commit. Whatever the player left unplanned inside a formation under orders
# is planned by that formation's executors, and the round plays out.
key Enter
until idle

# Pick the platoon up again to read its orders back: the mission has landed
# (MissionReceived in the log) so it is standing rather than travelling, and
# any member who has driven out of her leader's radius carries the tag.
key F
wait 0.8
log "one round fought; the order has arrived"
shot /tmp/command-05-standing.png

# A second round, to get the two halves of the command picture on screen at
# once: enemies somebody is watching are drawn solidly with their health,
# enemies nobody can see any more are dimmed ghosts on the hex they were last
# reported from.
key Enter
until idle
log "two rounds fought: fresh contacts and ghosts"
shot /tmp/command-06-picture.png

# The panel's side of the picture, and the order rule that goes with it: a
# ghost is a report, not a target, so pointing a gun at one is refused in the
# log rather than silently ignored. Where the fight leaves its ghosts depends
# on the seed, so this pair of hexes is written for STAHL_SEED=7 — the recon
# car, who was never given a mission and so is still where she deployed, and
# a hex a Valkyrie was last reported from two rounds earlier. On another seed
# it is an ordinary tile and an ordinary click.
hex 5,14
wait 0.4
click left
wait 0.5
hex 21,16
wait 0.5
click left
wait 0.8
log "a ghost hovered and a shot at it refused"
shot /tmp/command-07-ghost.png

quit
