# What a battle cost, on a page that stops for it.
#!env
#
#   STAHL_PRESENT=immediate STAHL_DEBUG=1 \
#     STAHL_SCRIPT=scripts/dev/after-action.txt cargo run -p stahlsenshamädchen
#
# No STAHL_BATTLE: this one starts on the campaign map, because the
# after-action report is a campaign screen and a scenario battle has no
# campaign to come back to.
#
# frontier puts Kuhlmann's 1st Company at offset [1, 1] -> axial (1, 1) and
# the 2nd Company at [1, 7] -> axial (-2, 7). Day one moves the 1st Company
# east; day two sends the 2nd Company down the southern highway, which is
# the road the Valkyrie Reserve comes up for the western factory. What day
# they meet on is the AI's business, so from there the tour waits on the
# game: `press Enter until` somebody engages, until the battle screen is
# listening, and until the campaign holds the screen with the report.
#
# The Enters that end the two scripted days are spaced by `wait` rather
# than `until idle`, which is the one place here a stopwatch is the right
# tool: they are keystrokes being spaced, not a simulation being waited on,
# and a campaign day settles in well under a second. Everything that is
# actually waiting on the game uses `until` or `press … until`.

at 3.0
shot /tmp/aa-01-campaign.png

# Day one: select 1st Company and march it four hexes east.
hex 1,1
wait 0.5
click left
wait 0.5
hex 5,1
wait 0.5
click left
wait 1.0
key Enter
wait 4.0

# Day two, from where she stopped.
# Day 2: 2nd Company down the southern highway toward the western factory.
# The Valkyrie Reserve comes up that road for the same factory, so the two
# meet on it within a few days. Everything about *when* is left to the
# loops below: the tour waits for the engagement rather than for a day.
hex -2,7
wait 0.5
click left
wait 0.5
hex 2,6
wait 0.5
click left
wait 1.0
key Enter
wait 4.0
# End turns until somebody engages somebody, then press through the muster
# prompt and every round of the battle until the campaign reports it.
press Enter until log "engages" 400
# Contact puts the muster prompt up when a neighbour could join, and Enter
# on it is "fight"; with nobody to ask the battle simply starts. Either way,
# press until the battle screen is listening, then commit rounds until the
# campaign holds the screen again, which is the after-action page — the
# battle screen never holds it on Enter.
press Enter until idle 60
press Enter until waiting 400

# ...and back to the campaign, where the bill is waiting. This is the shot
# this tour exists for: named cadets, what happened to each, and a campaign
# that has stopped to say so instead of narrating it past in among the tile
# income at three lines a second.
expect waiting
expect log "Day"
shot /tmp/aa-04-after-action.png

# One key dismisses it and the campaign runs on.
key Enter
until idle 30
shot /tmp/aa-05-dismissed.png

quit
